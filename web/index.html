    Verification scope and assumptions
    Mathematics
    Compiled solver
    Runtime boundary
    At most 16 congruences, signed i64 values, and two readable 128-byte arrays without pointer wrap. The mathematical finite-list theorems have no capacity limit.
    Execution guarantee
    A terminating, nontrapping normal execution exists; every normal result is correct. Universal termination of every reduction schedule is not claimed.
    Overflow behavior
    For valid inputs, incompatibility takes priority over overflow. The canonical residue and repeat period must fit signed i64 to be returned; the set of mathematical solutions is unbounded.
    Outside the proof
    The browser interface, JavaScript engine, and compiler translation. Generated admitted certificates are excluded from the trusted proof dependencies.
